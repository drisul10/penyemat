# Folded-corner signature

The brand's one motif: every saved item wears a folded top-right corner (22 px on cards, 12 px on rows, `clip-path` plus a fold-shadow triangle). When a page is saved, the corner folds in over 220 ms, ease-out; reduced motion shows it already folded.

- Tokens and motion: DESIGN.md direction block. Spec: design.html section 7.
