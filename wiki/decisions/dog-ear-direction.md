# Dog-ear visual direction

**Decision:** Marigold brand field, Zilla Slab + Work Sans, a folded corner on every saved item.

**Options considered:** 1 Dog-ear, 2 Index (search-led, cool), 3 Wayfinder (transit signage, dark).

**Why:** General users recognise the folded page corner as a bookmark without explanation.

**Cost:** Yellow over large areas tires the eye, so it is limited to the brand band and the save moment.

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-19.
