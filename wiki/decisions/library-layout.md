# Library layout: search field plus category shelves

**Decision:** A large search field in the brand band, then shelves of folded cards grouped by category.

**Options considered:** A Ask (search-first), B Timeline (grouped by day), C Shelves (grouped by site); the owner combined A with category shelves.

**Why:** Search is the reason to choose the product; shelves give a browsable fallback.

**Cost:** Shelves take more space than a list; a taller brand band pushes them down.

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-19.
