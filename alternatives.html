Library screen: three ways to find a link again

All three use the Dog-ear direction and the same sample bookmarks. They differ in what the user sees before typing, which is the open question: do people come back with words they remember, a time they remember, or a site they remember? Pick one by letter.

  1. A user finds a known link by typing a remembered phrase, results updating as they type.
  2. The search field is the first thing seen at desktop and at 390px.
  3. Each result shows the matched words in context, so it is recognised without opening it.
  4. Saving takes one click and confirms within 400 ms, with the fold.
  5. Marigold stays in the brand band and the save moment, never behind lists.
