8 · Why it looks like this

Search is the biggest thing on the pagePeople choose Penyemat for search, so the field sits in the brand band, centred, at 56 px tall. Fitts's law: the most-used control is the largest and easiest target.
Results show the words you rememberedEach result shows its matched words in the page text. Recognition over recall: people confirm the right link by seeing what they remembered, without opening it.
Shelves, not foldersCategories show as shelves of cards on one page, so browsing needs no clicks into folders. Each link has one shelf, so nothing appears twice (Gestalt common region: one group, one container).
Marigold only at the top and on saveYellow carries the brand in the band and the save popup, and never sits behind lists. Aesthetic-usability: the brand is felt without making long lists harder to read.
Delete has Undo, not a confirmDeleting one link is quick to reverse, so it happens at once with Undo. Deleting a category moves many links, so that one asks first. Nielsen's user control and freedom.
